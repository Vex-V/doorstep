# Footage

Each event's video in `clips/` is cut from one of these stock clips. None is
committed: `python scripts/demo_clips.py` downloads them from these pages (free
downloads) and makes the videos. The Pexels licence allows use in a video
without attribution and forbids selling the clips unaltered or showing an
identifiable person in a bad light.

**16:40:** in the demo he is only "a caller who wasn't expected", which is
what the alert detects. Don't call him a scammer or a burglar in the
voice-over: the licence forbids showing an identifiable person in a bad light.
Every close-up stranger clip we found was a delivery driver, whom the model
rightly calls a courier, so this one is filmed from further away than the rest.

**15:10:** stock libraries have almost no footage of a fall; this is someone
sliding off a sofa and lying on the floor, filmed as a wall camera would. It
ends with her still down, which is the picture the "still on the floor"
alert checks.
